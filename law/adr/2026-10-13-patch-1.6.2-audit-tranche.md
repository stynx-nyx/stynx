# ADR-RELEASE-0005 — STYNX 1.6.2 audit tranche

The Owner authorized the 1.6.2 plan on 2026-10-13: integrate complete,
reviewed evidence, inventory, test-admission and workflow slices as quickly as
the normal protected release controls allow. The Owner may call a cutoff at any
time; unfinished slices continue toward 1.6.3. The four upstream issue
closures remain with DETRAN adoption. Published 1.6.1 and its historical
observations remain unchanged.

The closed [1.6.2 scope](../policy/release-scope-1.6.2.json) is prospective and
applies only to the uniform 44-package 1.6.2 fixed group. It retains all
fifteen signed local RC nodes, including the existing cold-unit performance
task, and requires unique successful exact-candidate `verified-local-rc` with
independent exact-tree verification. No k6 or mutation tests are run. HTTP
performance is `NOT_OBSERVED`, mutation is `NOT_RUN`, and neither omitted
measurement promotes readiness. All ordinary formatting, lint, type, tests,
coverage, generated/API/trace, migration/RLS, security, package-boundary,
candidate-identity and publication checks remain required at their existing
thresholds. The candidate must bind the actual reviewed `test-tasks.json` bytes
and exact schema digest before the scope is used; a descriptor change requires
fresh review and rebinding, not an inferred pass.

The registry anomaly policy advances prospectively from verified published
1.6.1 to candidate 1.6.2. The prior Owner decisions, historical anomalous
`@stynx-nyx/angular-profile@2.0.0` artifact identity and its noncanonical
classification remain intact. A fresh authenticated complete 44-package
registry/GitHub census immediately before publication must confirm 1.6.1 as
latest, 1.6.2 absent, and the existing rc tag unchanged. The anomaly exception
is exact to 1.6.2 and grants no deletion, deprecation, replacement, dist-tag
mutation or scope expansion.

Integrate only complete slices through normal protected PRs. A workflow-file
commit needs its exact Owner `FORBID-CI-WITHOUT-ADR` receipt; a rebase landing
that rewrites the workflow commit needs a separate append-only receipt for the
landed SHA before release interval verification. The reviewed contracts-test
slice has been versioned through the normal Changesets flow as described below;
complete the remaining bindings, freeze one exact candidate and run the signed
fifteen-node RC against that candidate. Publication requires exact current
`main`, the explicit full `candidate_sha` and `publish:true` dispatch, Owner
repository opt-in, required token, and all ordinary action-specific receipts
and independent 44-artifact checks. A failed, partial or ambiguous actual
publication stops recovery mutation pending fresh exact Owner authorization.

Observe the actual publication-time scorecard without making it a release gate
or claiming unearned PASS. Preserve the 1.5.7 observation bytes and proof-chain
prefix and retain `readiness_promoting=false`. Rolling CI history and unfinished
test-idiomaticity repairs may continue into 1.6.3. No k6, mutation, threshold
weakening, test removal or synthetic history is authorized by this amendment.
