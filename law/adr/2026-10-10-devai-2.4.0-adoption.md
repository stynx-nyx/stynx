## Inventory production and custody

After a clean committed candidate and successful binding, use the registered
`devai sense run inventory_regeneration --repo-root . --as-role architect
--write --format json` action. The published producer resolves the checkout
root before portability checks, captures candidate head/tree and admits the
candidate's source files. It stages the aggregate inventory and then produces,
in order, API, Angular routes, data model, RBAC, data handling, dependency graph
and coverage. RBAC consumes the newly staged model/API; data handling consumes
the model; coverage consumes the API/routes. Successful publication is atomic;
a failed generator or changed source snapshot must retain/refuse custody
according to the actual producer result, never a hand-patched body.

The aggregate `.devai/state/inventory/inventory.json` and `law/trace.json` are the
inventory-adherence inputs. Run the registered `inventory_adherence` sensor
only after regeneration, with the same unchanged candidate and fresh trace
binding. Require schema validity, exact integration head and the actual
adherence result. No invented `inventory_adherence` policy field is permitted
by the published closed schema. Preserve producer REVIEW and errors; successful
file generation alone does not imply coverage or adherence PASS.

Inspector exercises the installed package for repeat observations, candidate
custody, root portability, tampered pins, genuine path leakage and failed
publication rollback. Auditor independently verifies append-only observation
custody and the unchanged historical 1.5.7 observation bytes/proof-chain prefix.
No observation is deleted, rewritten, relabeled or treated as current merely
because the dependency upgraded. Keep `readiness_promoting=false`.

## Explicit remaining gaps

The initial projection retains `type_check.argv=["pnpm","typecheck"]`. The
published broker refuses that legacy shape; it remains unmeasured pending the
reviewed exact task binding. Replacing it with a different recursive command
would change the reviewed execution population and is not this repair.

Published 2.4.0 pattern walkers currently accept only `.test`/`.spec` with
`ts`, `tsx`, `js` or `jsx` extensions. STYNX's executable taxonomy also includes
`.e2e.ts`, `.wiring-spec.ts`, `.test.mjs`, `.spec.mjs` and `.test.cjs`. Expanding
locations does not repair these suffix omissions. The existing complete STYNX
trace corpus remains required; do not rename tests or claim the sensor measured
the whole corpus until its producer contract is repaired and independently
regressed.

New task references require `sensorKinds` plus an exact output population in
`test-tasks.json`. The independently installed published DEVAI 1.9.0 protected
RC verifier rejects `sensorKinds` as `SCHEMA_INVALID`. Preserve that refusal
until the trusted verifier and its exact package/workflow bindings support the
reviewed schema through normal controls. The published test-output parser also
expects unprefixed test summaries; ordinary Turbo-prefixed summaries are not
an admitted executed-test population merely by selecting `kind=vitest`.

These gaps require subsequent supported producer/consumer integration. This
initial adoption supplies neither fabricated task evidence nor green scorecard
claims. Full 43 PASS plus the two existing N/A cells, ordinary gates and exact
candidate release controls remain the campaign's unchanged acceptance target.
