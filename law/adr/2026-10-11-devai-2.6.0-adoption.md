`init upgrade --target . --as-role architect --format json`, then inspect its

binding, HMAC and receipt check. The refresh does not switch adapter identity,
claim simultaneous local enforcement or grant a merge receipt.

refusal remains a refusal; this decision is not permission to alter installed
