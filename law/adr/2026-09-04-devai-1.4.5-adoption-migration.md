# ADR-DEVAI-ADOPTION-0002 — Retire local governance and adopt the installed DEVAI 1.4.5 contract

**Status:** Accepted.
**Authority:** Architect, under the Owner's explicit direction to ditch STYNX's
local governance machinery and adopt the published `@aarusso-nyx/devai` package.
**Supersedes:** `2026-08-16-trusted-local-rc-evidence.md`,
`2026-08-24-stynx-1.1.1-campaign-controls.md`.
**Amends:** `ADR-DEVAI-ADOPTION-0001`.

## Context

STYNX carried a complete second governance implementation alongside the
installed DEVAI package: local release-candidate orchestration, a candidate
campaign policy and schema, a mutation evidence composition and reuse engine, a
direct verifier invocation, and two dedicated workflows. STYNX is a framework
product, not a governance platform. Maintaining both paths duplicated DEVAI's
responsibilities and bound release readiness to one specific 1.1.1 candidate.

The Owner selected `@aarusso-nyx/devai@1.5.0` as the adoption target. That
version is **not published**; the resolving registry
(`https://npm.pkg.github.com`) ends at `1.4.5`. The Owner then authorized
adopting `1.4.5`, which STYNX already depends on and is already bound to
(`.devai/config/authority-policy.json`, materialized 2026-08-31,
`devai doctor` passing at tier1).

## Decision

### D1. DEVAI owns the generic governance machinery

STYNX uses the installed package's supported interfaces and does not reimplement
adoption validation, release planning, preflight, certification, evidence
contracts, authorization enforcement, or offline verification.

### D2. STYNX retains only product-specific declarations and behavior

The package roster, mutation targets and thresholds, task graph, product ADRs,
tenancy and RLS rules, database definitions, and public API compatibility remain
STYNX responsibilities.

### D3. Mutation execution stays with STYNX; composition moves to DEVAI

DEVAI's `test:mutation` task node invokes `pnpm run test:mutation` under
`runner: stryker-v1` and discovers the report set through its
`mutation-report-set-discovery-v1` output contract. DEVAI compares reports
against thresholds; it does not execute Stryker. `scripts/run-mutation-evidence.mjs`
is therefore retained as a product executor, reduced from 2068 to 380 lines,
with all composition, reuse, focused-mode, and candidate-binding logic removed.

The complete 38-package mutation population and the `break: 90` acceptance floor
are unchanged. This decision does not alter D3 of
`2026-05-21-mutation-thresholds-tiered.md`.

### D4. Product requirements are extracted before their carriers are retired

Two still-valid product requirements were embedded in the retired campaign
policy and are preserved as STYNX-owned policy:

| Requirement | New home |
| --- | --- |
| 44/38/6 package census and approved first publications | `law/policy/stynx-package-roster.json` |
| Exact adopted DEVAI package identity | `law/policy/devai-package-identity.json` |

The roster's 38 mutation packages were verified byte-identical to filesystem
discovery. The identity pin was verified against `node_modules` and
`pnpm-lock.yaml`.

### D5. Revived decisions are consistent with current behavior

`2026-08-16-trusted-local-rc-evidence.md` recorded that it superseded, on
cutover, decisions D2 and D4 of `2026-05-21-mutation-thresholds-tiered.md`.
Retiring it revives both, and both remain accurate:

- **D2** (`STRYKER_INCREMENTAL` split mode): the mutation child environment
  still forces `STRYKER_INCREMENTAL=false`, asserted by
  `test/scripts/validate.js`.
- **D4** (per-PR mutation gating postponed): mutation still runs as a
  dependency-gated task, not a per-PR gate.

No threshold, tier assignment, or package population changes as a result.

### D6. Retired governance is preserved as inactive history

The superseded ADRs keep their accepted bytes unchanged and remain in
`law/adr` as history. They confer no current authority. `law/trace.json` is
retained as an inactive historical record; no executable path reads it.

## Consequences

Removed as active governance, with pre-removal digests recorded in
`law/policy/retired-governance-catalog.json`:

- local RC orchestration and its environment, toolchain, and trust-store pins
- the 1.1.1 campaign policy and schema, and the mutation reuse policy
- the direct verifier invocation and its task-graph node
- the local RC verification and main observation workflows
- the mutation composition, reuse, and focused-evidence engines

Test populations are preserved under the porting rule. Retirements and their
replacements are recorded in
`docs/meta/migration/stynx-1.2.0-devai-adoption-census.md`.

## Known limits

The installed 1.4.5 action catalog has 48 actions and contains no `prepare` and
no `export` action. The achievable lifecycle is
`plan → preflight → certify` through `devai check --release-stage`, followed by
`export → offline-verify` through the `devai-evidence-export` and
`devai-evidence-bundle-verify` binaries, which sit outside the governed action
catalog. Export additionally requires `--private-key`, `--public-key`, and
`--signer-id`. Those control identities are not held by this migration and must
be supplied by the Owner before any candidate export.
