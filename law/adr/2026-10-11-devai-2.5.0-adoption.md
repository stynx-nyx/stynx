## Trusted verifier and remote boundary

The published generator advances the trusted verifier from 1.9.0 to the already
published `@aarusso-nyx/devai@2.4.0`. The required canonical verifier provenance
SHA-256 is
`fc79da07d4d4dfa466548337a4944ae1b7e0bfdcf2f1c334b341bb1ad477cd4d`.
Preserve signer trust, exact-tree transport, inert candidate handling and all
integrity/evidence checks. Regenerate through the registered upgrade; an exact
Owner `FORBID-CI-WITHOUT-ADR` receipt is required for the actual resulting
workflow commit, and a separate exact landed receipt after a rebase merge when
its SHA changes. The existing direct mandate authorizes the necessary action;
a receipt accurately binds that authority to an observed SHA, without claiming
the Owner previously spoke an unknown literal SHA.

The migration describes a verifier-only update for repositories with an existing
adopter-owned `.devai/config/toolchain.json` override. STYNX has no such override;
the published canonical defaults already select verifier 2.4.0 and the initial
plan is ready. Do not create or hand-edit that file. If the actual supported
operation refuses, preserve the refusal and resolve the concrete interface gap.

Before any remote verification, Engineer independently verifies and updates the
effective repository and applicable environment value of
`DEVAI_LEDGER_VERIFIER_PROVENANCE_SHA256` through normal protected controls.
Generated workflow bytes and local policy do not update that value. This
Architect checkpoint performs no protected-setting change, push, remote
execution, integration or publication. Keep those operations separately bound
to their actual candidate and authority.

## Supported interfaces require real adopter evidence

Published 2.5.0 adds closed optional `invariant_producers` and
`reviewed_workflow_steps` documents. The former binds exact invariant/subject,
task, population and required test identities; the latter binds exact canonical
YAML occurrences, reviewed effects and complete executable/import closure.
Registered adopter binding owns each whole target independently with validated
source/target digests, atomic rollback, unmanaged-absence preservation and
canonical-empty retirement after verified prior ownership. Neither schema-valid
JSON nor generated files prove candidate-dependent coverage or execution.

Native Vitest/Playwright `resultPopulation` requires complete tracked selection
and collection plus final results from the same admitted execution, exact
candidate/task/environment/runner/reporter custody and its private host-issued
execution-origin receipt. Authored or cloned result JSON, skips, cache replay,
missing or mismatched tests and stale populations cannot earn executed PASS.
Node TAP remains diagnostic UNKNOWN. Retain all task/population heads with worst
outcome winning; one passing population cannot hide a missing or failing sibling.

The published suffix/glob, qualified endpoint, semantic mock and workflow
classification improvements remain subject to installed regression and fresh
candidate observations. They do not make any historical failure disappear.
The initial source retains legacy `type_check.argv=["pnpm","typecheck"]` and
omits native task bindings. These remain explicit unmeasured obligations until
reviewed exact descriptor/output contracts and independent tests are supplied.
The supported annotations and verifier repin remove an upstream schema barrier;
they do not manufacture the missing measurements. Preserve every mandatory
ordinary task, full source/test population and complete HTTP performance
composition/fixture/SLO/workload requirement in ADR-PERF-0001.
